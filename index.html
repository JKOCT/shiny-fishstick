Jobs: use + and - to put villagers to work: chopping, mining, fishing or cooking
Food: work and walking make villagers hungry. Fishers catch fish, cooks turn them into meals
Build: pick a building and tap to place it. Villagers build it
Remove: tap a site, road or building to take it down
Look around: drag with Look, right-drag, WASD / arrows, two fingers, or tap the minimap
Zoom: wheel, pinch, or +/-
Pet Little Wolf: tap them with Look
Keys: 1-7 tools, Esc = Look, M = mute
